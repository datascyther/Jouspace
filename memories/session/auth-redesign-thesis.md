# Auth Redesign — Design Thesis (Phase 0)

Jouspace's auth experience should feel like stepping into a quiet, private room — not signing up for another app.

The visual promise is craft and intent: generous negative space, tactile inputs, and a single confident purple gradient that says "this space was made for your thoughts, and only your thoughts."

A premium auth screen earns trust through restraint — every element earns its place, motion reveals rather than decorates, and privacy is presented as a feature to admire rather than a clause to accept.
